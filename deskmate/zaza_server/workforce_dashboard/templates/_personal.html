What ZaZa records

When monitoring is configured, ZaZa records computer activity state, application/window metadata according to the configured privacy policy, work-session/attendance summaries, and device connectivity.

ZaZa does not collect screenshots, screen/OCR contents, typed key contents, clipboard contents, webcam/video, or microphone/audio.

Computer activity is not proof of work or a productivity score. This product information does not replace your employer's privacy notice.

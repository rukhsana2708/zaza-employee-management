Schedules use each employee's own time zone. A shift whose end is earlier than its start ends the next day and belongs to the day it starts. A one-date rule overrides the weekly rule for that date. Changes apply from today (the employee's local date) onwards; past days keep their schedule. Correcting a past schedule is an administrator task (command line, followed by a recalculation).
